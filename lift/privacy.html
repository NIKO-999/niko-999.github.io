Lift privacy policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

The short version

Lift keeps your workouts on your phone. We do not have accounts, we do not collect your email, and there are no ads or trackers. Nothing leaves your phone unless you turn on Friends.

What stays on your phone

Your workouts, exercises, weights, reps, body weight, notes, photos, goals, settings and badges are stored on your device only. We cannot see them. If you delete the app, they are deleted.

Friends (optional, off by default)

If you turn on Friends, Lift sends a small summary to our server so your friends can see it. This is:

  • the name you choose
  • how often and how much you trained, and a 30 day strip of which days you trained
  • your goal, streak, personal record counts and cardio minutes
  • reactions you send to friends
  • if you turn on live notifications, your phone's notification address, so we can tell you when a friend reacts

It never includes the weights you lifted, which exercises you did, your body weight, food, photos or anything you typed in your notes.

You are identified by a random code and a random secret key made on your phone. There is no email or phone number tied to it. Your friend list is kept on your phone, not on our server.

Where it is stored and for how long

Friends data is stored with Cloudflare (a Workers key value store). It is deleted automatically after 60 days without an update.

Deleting your data

In Lift, open Friends and tap Turn off friends. This deletes your data from our server straight away. Deleting the app removes everything on your phone.

Other things

  • Lift opens exercise videos on YouTube only when you tap a link to do so. That is YouTube's own page and its own policy.
  • We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising or tracking.
  • Lift is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes. Questions: open an issue at github.com/NIKO-999/niko-999.github.io/issues.
