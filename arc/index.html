    Backup

    Your goals live in this browser on this machine and are never uploaded anywhere. That is the point — but it also means clearing your browsing data deletes them, and another computer will not have them. A copy is a plain JSON file you can keep anywhere and read yourself.

    Photographs are not in the copy. They are held separately, and can be several megabytes each — a backup file you cannot email is one you will not keep. The goals are what is irreplaceable.
