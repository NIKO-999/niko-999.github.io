Fig. 1 — General arrangement

The apparatus comprises sixty-four (64) plates carried upon a common spindle, in the order of the solar wheel.

Four plates of increased diameter are fitted according to the subject's date of birth and are identified by callout: Life's Work, taken at the sun; Evolution, opposite; Radiance, taken 88° prior; and Purpose, opposite that.

Selecting the assembly separates it along the spindle; the fitted plates are then marked by pointer. Selecting a marked plate withdraws it for inspection at Fig. 2.

Plates not fitted to this assembly remain available for inspection and are described in general terms only.
