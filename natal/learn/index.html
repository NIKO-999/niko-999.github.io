How to read the sky you were born under

A birth chart is a map of where the Sun, Moon and planets were at the moment you were born, seen from the place you were born. This guide explains every part of it in plain English, from the twelve signs to transits and relationship charts, so you can open your own chart and know what you are looking at.

01

What a birth chart is

Picture yourself standing where you were born, at the minute you were born, looking up. The Sun, the Moon and each planet sat somewhere against the background of the stars. A birth chart records those positions and turns them into a circle you can read.

Every chart is built from three pieces of information: the date, which sets where the planets were; the time, which sets which part of the sky was rising and so fixes the houses; and the place, because the sky looks different from Whangārei than from Perth at the same moment.

The time matters more than most people expect. The Ascendant, the sign rising on the eastern horizon, moves about one degree every four minutes and changes sign roughly every two hours. The Moon moves about half a degree an hour. A birth time that is out by even ten minutes can shift the Ascendant and the house cusps. If you do not know your time, the app casts the chart for noon: the planets' signs and most aspects are still right, but the houses and the Ascendant are left out.

Reading a chart comes down to four questions, and the next four chapters answer them in turn: what is acting (the planets), how it acts (the signs), where in life it acts (the houses), and how the parts talk to each other (the aspects).

A sentence to remember

A planet is a part of you, its sign is its style, its house is the stage it plays on, and its aspects are the conversations it has with the rest of you. "Venus in Gemini in the 10th house" reads as: your way of loving and valuing (Venus) is curious and talkative (Gemini) and shows up in your career and public life (10th house).

02

Planets and points

The planets are the actors in the chart. The Sun and Moon, called the luminaries, are the core of who you are. Mercury, Venus and Mars are the personal planets: fast-moving, and different even for people born a few weeks apart. Jupiter and Saturn are the social planets, shaping how you grow and where you meet your limits. Uranus, Neptune and Pluto move so slowly that a whole generation shares their signs, so their houses and aspects say more about you than their signs do.

Points and bodies

Not everything in a chart is a planet. The lunar nodes are the two points where the Moon's path crosses the Sun's; astrologers read them as the direction of growth (North Node) and the comfort zone you arrive with (South Node). Chiron is a small body between Saturn and Uranus, Lilith is a point in the Moon's orbit, and the Part of Fortune and Vertex are calculated from the Sun, Moon and Ascendant.

☊︎

North Node

destiny · growth · direction · future · cycle 18.6 years

The North Node (Rahu) is the point where the Moon's orbit crosses the ecliptic heading north. It points toward unfamiliar territory your soul is growing into this lifetime.

☋︎

past · gifts · comfort zone · release · cycle 18.6 years

The South Node (Ketu) is the karmic past: talents and habits already mastered. It is comfortable, but over-relying on it keeps you from growing.

Chiron, the wounded healer, marks the place of an early, tender hurt that never fully closes, and the wisdom that lets you heal others through it.

⚸︎

shadow · raw power · rejection · autonomy · cycle 8.85 years

Black Moon Lilith (the mean lunar apogee) marks exiled, rejected parts of the self (raw desire, anger and autonomy) that reclaim their power when owned.

luck · ease · prosperity · joy

The Part of Fortune blends Sun, Moon and Ascendant. It shows where happiness and material well-being come most naturally.

Vx

Vertex

The Vertex, on the western side of the chart, is often active in fated meetings and turning points that feel beyond your control.

Vesta, keeper of the hearth flame, shows what you give yourself to wholeheartedly, where you need solitude to stay focused, and how you keep your inner fire alight.

03

Signs, elements and modes

The zodiac is a circle of twelve signs, each 30° wide, starting at the point where the Sun crosses the equator in March. A sign is a style: it colours how any planet in it behaves. Each sign combines an element, which describes its temperament, with a mode, which describes how it moves, and each has a ruling planet that acts as its caretaker.

Fire

Enthusiasm, courage and inspiration drive you. You act on faith and energise others. Aries, Leo, Sagittarius.

Earth

You are practical, grounded and reliable. You need results you can touch. Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn.

Air

You live through ideas, words and connection. Objectivity is your gift. Gemini, Libra, Aquarius.

Water

Emotion, intuition and empathy guide you. You understand what is unspoken. Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces.

Cardinal

You are an initiator: quick to start, lead and change direction. Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn begin each season.

Fixed

You are a stabiliser: loyal, determined and able to see things through. Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius hold the middle of each season.

Mutable

You are an adapter: flexible, curious and good at transitions. Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces close each season and prepare the next.

The builder of the zodiac. Taurus slows the spark of Aries into something tangible: a body, a home, a resource that lasts.

Tropical or sidereal

Western astrology, and this app by default, uses the tropical zodiac, tied to the seasons. Vedic astrology uses the sidereal zodiac, tied to the stars, which currently sits about 24° behind. The app can show either in its settings.

04

Houses

The houses divide the sky around your birthplace into twelve areas of life. They are counted anticlockwise from the Ascendant, the point rising on the eastern horizon when you were born. Four points matter most. The Ascendant (start of the 1st house) is how you meet the world. The IC (start of the 4th) is your roots and private life. The Descendant (start of the 7th) is what you meet in partners. The Midheaven (start of the 10th) is your public direction and calling.

A house with planets in it is a busy area of life. An empty house is not an inactive one: it is read through the sign on its cusp and wherever that sign's ruling planet sits.

House systems

There are several ways to divide the sky into houses: by time, by space or into equal slices. The app defaults to Placidus, the most widely used system and the one Astro-Seek uses, and also offers Koch, Regiomontanus, Porphyry, Equal, Whole Sign, Topocentric, Campanus. Whole Sign gives each house one complete sign, as in Hellenistic astrology. Near the poles some systems break down, and the app switches to Porphyry.

When a sign sits entirely inside a house without touching a cusp, it is intercepted. The app lists intercepted signs on the house page; their qualities are read as held back or slower to develop.

Aspects

Aspects are the angles between planets, measured around the circle. When two planets sit at one of a few significant angles, they work together, whether smoothly or with friction. The orb is how far from exact an aspect can be and still count: the tighter the orb, the stronger the aspect. The app uses the same orbs as Astro-Seek, with wider allowances for the Sun and Moon.

Harmonious

Trines and sextiles connect planets in compatible elements. This is a supportive link: use it deliberately and it becomes one of your greatest assets.

Challenging

Squares and oppositions connect planets that pull against each other. This is a growth edge. Friction here pushes you to integrate two parts of yourself that do not naturally agree.

Blending

Conjunctions put two planets in the same place. These energies are inseparable in you; which way they express depends on the planets involved.

Applying and separating

An aspect is applying when the faster planet is still moving towards exactness, and separating when it has passed. Applying aspects are read as building and forward-moving; separating ones as familiar and already integrated. Each aspect in the app is labelled one or the other.

Declination: parallels and out of bounds

Signs measure position along the zodiac. Declination measures how far north or south of the celestial equator a planet sits. Two planets at the same declination form a parallel, which works like a conjunction; at mirror declinations they form a contra-parallel, which works like an opposition. A planet beyond the Sun's furthest reach, about 23.4°, is out of bounds and is read as acting in an independent, unusual or extreme way. The app marks these on the Planets tab.

Aspect patterns

When several aspects lock together they form a pattern, a larger shape in the chart that works as a single unit.

Grand Trine

Three planets form an equilateral triangle of trines: a closed circuit of natural talent and ease. It is protective and gifted, but can breed complacency unless consciously put to work.

A grand trine links three planets in the same element, each one supporting the other two, so energy flows round the circuit without friction. Living with it feels like having a room in yourself where things simply work. Talents arrive early, certain moods come easily, and they are easy to overlook as gifts because they have never cost you anything.

T-Square

Two planets in opposition both square a third, the apex. The apex planet is a pressure point and a source of tremendous drive: this is where you are pushed to act.

A T-square forms when two planets oppose each other and both square a third, the apex. It is one of the most dynamic patterns in a chart, and living with it can feel like being permanently under construction. The opposition sets up a tug of war, and all that tension pours into the apex planet, which becomes the pressure point where you keep acting, pushing and reacting.

Grand Cross

Four planets locked in two oppositions and four squares. Strength and stamina are built through constant, many-sided challenge.

A grand cross joins four planets in two oppositions and four squares, usually all in the same modality. It is a heavy, load-bearing pattern. It feels like being pulled in four directions at once, with every move in one area creating pressure somewhere else, and there is no easy exit because the cross has no open side to release through.

Yod · Finger of God

Two planets in sextile both quincunx a third, the apex. Traditionally a karmic, fated configuration: the apex planet points to a special mission that requires ongoing adjustment.

A yod forms when two planets sextile each other and both make a quincunx to a third, creating a long narrow triangle sometimes called the finger of fate. It carries an awkward, restless quality. The quincunx links planets that share nothing natural, so the adjustments it asks for never feel quite finished.

Stellium

Three or more planets clustered in one sign or house concentrate life energy there: an area of intense focus and experience.

A stellium is a cluster of three or more planets gathered in one sign or house. It concentrates a great deal of energy in one place, so that part of life becomes the centre of gravity for your whole personality. Others notice it quickly, even when you do not, because it colours everything you do.

06

Reading strength

Not every planet speaks equally loudly. A few factors tell you which parts of a chart dominate and how comfortably each planet works.

Dignity

Domicile

The planet rules the sign, for example Mars in Aries or Venus in Libra. The planet is at home in this sign. Its qualities flow naturally and powerfully.

Exaltation

A sign where the planet is especially well placed, for example the Sun in Aries or the Moon in Taurus. The planet is an honoured guest in this sign. It expresses at its most refined.

Detriment

The sign opposite its home, for example Venus in Aries. The planet is in the sign opposite its home. It has to work harder and often develops unusual strength.

Fall

The sign opposite its exaltation, for example the Moon in Scorpio. The planet is opposite its exaltation. Its expression can feel awkward until it is consciously developed.

Retrograde planets

Seen from Earth, a planet appears to stop and move backwards for a while when Earth overtakes it or it overtakes Earth. A planet that was retrograde at your birth is read as turning its themes inward: they are processed privately, revisited, and developed in your own way rather than the expected one. The app marks retrograde planets with ℞. Mercury is retrograde for about three weeks, three times a year; the outer planets are retrograde for months every year, so many people have one or more.

Critical degrees

At 29°: the anaretic degree. There is urgency and a sense of completion here: lessons of this sign culminate, and mastery feels required rather than optional. Often considered a karmic degree. At 0°: a fresh, raw expression of the sign, pure and a little untested.

Dominant planets and chart shape

Planetary dominance is worked out by scoring each planet on several measures of strength. The Sun and Moon earn weight as the luminaries, and the planet ruling your Ascendant earns extra as chart ruler. Planets in angular houses or close to the angles score highly, as do planets in their own sign or exaltation. Tight aspects, and ruling the signs that hold other planets, add further points. The result shows which planetary voices speak loudest in your personality, and which are quieter, helping you understand your natural strengths and the areas that need conscious development.

The overall spread of planets around the wheel also has a shape. The seven classic shapes are Bundle, Bowl, Bucket, Locomotive, Seesaw, Splash, Splay. A bowl chart, for example, has every planet in one half of the wheel and is read as self-contained and purposeful. The app names your chart's shape on the Chart tab.

The Moon phase you were born in

The angle between the Sun and Moon at your birth gives your Moon phase, one of eight stages in the monthly cycle from New Moon to Balsamic.

New Moon from 0°

Born at the new moon: instinctive, subjective and future-oriented. You are here to start something new and project yourself into life.

Crescent from 45°

Born in the crescent phase: you push forward against the pull of the past, building something with determination.

First Quarter from 90°

Born at the first quarter: a crisis-in-action personality. You thrive on challenge and on building strong structures.

Gibbous from 135°

Born in the gibbous phase: you are a refiner and analyst, driven to perfect and to find meaning.

Full Moon from 180°

Born at the full moon: relationship-oriented and highly aware. Life brings illumination through others and fulfilment of purpose.

Disseminating from 225°

Born in the disseminating phase: a teacher and communicator who shares what has been learned.

Last Quarter from 270°

Born at the last quarter: a crisis-in-consciousness personality: you reorient values and quietly lead change.

Balsamic from 315°

Born in the balsamic phase: a prophetic, old-soul energy. You complete cycles and prepare the seeds of the future. Often considered a karmic birth.

07

Karmic astrology

Karmic astrology reads the chart as a story that continues from before this life and points somewhere beyond it. In practice it describes two things: the habits you fall back on, and the direction of growth that feels unfamiliar but right.

The lunar nodes are its spine. The South Node shows gifts and patterns you arrive with, comfortable to the point of being a trap. The North Node, always opposite, shows the direction of growth. Saturn shows the lessons you meet through effort and time. Chiron marks a tender wound that becomes wisdom you can offer others. Lilith points to what was exiled or shamed and wants to be reclaimed. Pluto shows where you are asked to transform, and the 4th, 8th and 12th houses hold inherited, shared and hidden material.

Life cycles

Saturn return · about 29, 58, 88

Saturn comes back to its birth position. A reckoning with maturity: what you are really committed to holds, and what was built to please others falls away.

Nodal return · about 19, 37, 56

The North Node returns to its birth position. Doors open toward your direction of growth, and people or events arrive that feel fated.

Nodal reversal · about 9, 28, 46

The nodes swap places over your natal nodes. Old patterns resurface so you can release what no longer fits.

Chiron return · about 50

Chiron completes its orbit. It is read as the point where an old hurt turns into wisdom you can share, and purpose shifts from proving to meaning.

08

Transits and timing

A birth chart is fixed. The sky is not. Astrology has several ways to see how the moving sky interacts with the chart you were born with.

Transits

A transit is a planet in today's sky making an aspect to a planet in your birth chart. When transiting Saturn squares your natal Sun, for example, it is read as pressure around identity and direction for as long as Saturn is within orb. Fast planets bring passing moods; slow ones bring chapters. As a transiting planet moves through each of your houses, it also colours that area of life. The app shows transits for any day, week or month, with the dates they become exact, plus New and Full Moons, sign changes and retrograde stations.

Secondary progressions

Secondary progressions are one of the oldest timing tools in astrology, built on a simple rule: each day after your birth stands for one year of your life. The sky on the day you turned ten days old describes the inner weather of your tenth year, the sky thirty days after birth describes your thirtieth, and so on. Because the planets move slowly by this measure, the progressed chart unfolds gently, like a photograph developing over decades.

The progressed Moon is the one to watch: it changes sign about every two and a half years and marks clear emotional chapters. The progressed Sun changes sign about every thirty years.

Solar returns

A solar return chart is cast for the exact moment each year when the Sun comes back to the degree and minute it held when you were born. That moment falls on or within a day of your birthday, and the chart drawn for it works as a snapshot of the year ahead. It shows the mood, the main arenas of life and the kinds of experiences likely to stand out until your next birthday.

Where you spend your birthday changes the return's houses and Ascendant, which is why the app lets you choose the place.

09

Relationships

Synastry compares two birth charts by laying one over the other and noticing where each person's planets touch the other's. A contact between your Venus and their Mars, or your Moon and their Saturn, describes a particular current running between you: where you light each other up, where you feel at home, and where you rub against each other. It does not describe either of you alone. It describes what happens in the space you share.

Synastry looks at two things. Inter-aspects are the aspects between one person's planets and the other's: Venus meeting Mars speaks to attraction, the Moon meeting the Sun to emotional fit, Saturn contacts to commitment and weight. House overlays show where one person's planets land in the other's houses, describing which areas of life the relationship stirs.

What the compatibility scores measure

Attraction

This measures the pull between you: the chemistry, desire and fascination that draws you together and keeps you curious. It weighs Venus and Mars contacts most heavily, along with Sun and Moon links and the intensity of any Pluto contacts.

Emotional

This measures how safe, understood and cared for you feel with each other. It draws mainly on Moon contacts, especially Moon with Venus and Moon with Sun, which show whether your instinctive needs fit together.

Mind

This measures how easily you talk, think and solve problems together. It is built from Mercury contacts, which show whether your minds click, spark or talk past each other.

Stability

This measures the capacity for commitment, loyalty and building something that lasts. It leans on Saturn contacts, the overall harmony between Sun and Moon, and Juno contacts where they are present.

Growth

This measures how much you expand, heal and move toward your purpose through each other. It draws on Jupiter, North Node and Chiron contacts, which show where the relationship stretches both of you in meaningful directions.

Friction

This measures the friction you are likely to meet: the squares and oppositions that create pressure, especially those involving Mars, Saturn, Pluto and Uranus. A high score is not a warning to leave, it shows where the relationship will ask the most of you.

Using the app

Enter a date, time and place of birth and the app casts the chart. Everything is calculated on your device, and charts are saved on your device only. Across the top (or down the side on a computer) are nine tabs. Tap any row, card or planet in a tab to open its full reading; the Back arrow returns you.

Chart

The wheel, your Sun, Moon and Ascendant, the signs your planets occupy, the balance of elements and modes, life areas (love, career, home and so on), and your chart signature: chart ruler, dominant planet and sign, chart shape, Moon phase, sect, hemispheres, retrograde planets and aspect patterns. Tap a planet on the wheel to trace its aspects.

Transits

Where the planets are today and the aspects they make to your chart. Switch between Day, Week and Month: week and month views show a calendar of exact transits, New and Full Moons, sign changes and retrograde stations, with the date each one happens.

Progressed

Your secondary progressions for any date: a second wheel around your natal chart, the progressed Sun, Moon, Ascendant and Midheaven, progressed aspects with their exact dates, the progressed Moon's timeline and your progressed Sun's life chapters.

Return

Your solar return for any year: the exact moment and a full chart for the year, with its themes (the rising sign, where the Sun and Moon fall, the ruler of the year) and every planet's placement. You can change the place to where you spend your birthday.

Synastry

Compare two charts. Add a person or choose a saved chart to see both on one wheel, every aspect between you, where each person's planets fall in the other's houses, and compatibility scores for attraction, emotion, communication, stability, growth and friction.

Planets

Every planet, point and asteroid with its sign, degree, house and dignity, the angles, and each planet's declination with out-of-bounds status and parallels. A breakdown shows which planets, signs, houses, elements and modes dominate.

Houses

The four angles, where your planets gather, and all twelve houses with the sign on each cusp, where its ruling planet sits and which planets are inside.

Aspects

A grid of every aspect in the chart and a full list with orbs and whether each is applying or separating, filterable by flowing, dynamic and conjunction, plus declination aspects.

Karmic

The lunar nodes and their axis, planets touching the nodes, Saturn, Chiron, Pluto and Lilith, retrograde planets, the 4th, 8th and 12th houses, and a timeline of your Saturn returns, nodal returns and reversals and Chiron return.

Settings

House system

Placidus by default, as on Astro-Seek. Koch, Whole Sign, Equal, Porphyry, Regiomontanus, Topocentric and Campanus are also available.

Zodiac

Tropical by default. Sidereal uses the Lahiri ayanamsa, as in Vedic astrology.

Lunar nodes

Mean node by default, as on Astro-Seek. The true node follows the Moon's actual orbit and differs by up to about 1.5°.

Aspects and orbs

Major aspects with Astro-Seek's orbs by default. Minor aspects can be added, and orbs set tighter or wider.

Asteroids

Ceres, Pallas, Juno and Vesta are shown by default and can be hidden.

Unknown birth time

Tick Unknown and the chart is cast for noon. Planet signs and most aspects stay accurate; houses, the Ascendant and the Midheaven are not shown.

How accurate the app is

The app calculates everything itself, in your browser, and has been checked against the Swiss Ephemeris, the reference engine behind professional astrology software and Astro-Seek. Over 400 test charts across Australia and New Zealand from 1900 to 2100, the largest differences were:

PointsLargest difference
Sun to Pluto0.32′

Mean node, Lilith0.04′
Ascendant, Midheaven, Vertex0.12′
House cusps, all eight systems0.07′
Ceres, Pallas, Juno, Vesta0.81′

One arc-minute (′) is a sixtieth of a degree, so these are fractions of a sixtieth. A reference chart checked against Astro-Seek matches it exactly: every position, house cusp, daily speed and aspect, including the orbs and whether each aspect is applying or separating. Time zones, including historical daylight saving, come from your device's official time-zone data.
