# GOLDEN STANDARD, PART 2 — Address & Reading Dynamics

## How "you" works
"You" is the default subject of nearly every sentence. The reader is being
spoken to, never described.

- Never "the user," never "a person with this Arcana," never "people who have
  this tend to."
- Never hedge the address itself: not "you might be someone who…" — just
  "you…". The hedging, where it belongs, is about the SYSTEM's claims, not
  about who they are.
- Don't stack "you" mechanically at the head of every sentence. Vary where it
  lands: "What you'd call just messing around is, more often than you think,
  actual craft."
- Second person for the reader; third person for anyone else in the reading
  (a partner, a parent, a lineage). Where someone's gender isn't established,
  use they/them.
- Never instruct from above. "Notice whether…" and "Ask yourself…" — not
  "You must" or "You need to."

## The central dynamic: validate → illuminate → invite
Every reading moves through this arc, in this order. It is the spine of the
whole thing.

1. VALIDATE — name the pattern as a real capacity or a survival strategy that
   made sense. The reader must feel recognised before they feel challenged.
2. ILLUMINATE — show the same energy misfiring. Not a different flaw: the SAME
   thing, seen from its cost side.
3. INVITE — open a door. Never assign homework, never diagnose.

Mastery validates. Shadow illuminates. Invitation invites. That's why shadow
must open fresh rather than as a "but" — it isn't a rebuttal of mastery, it's
the same energy viewed from the other side.

## The emotional arc inside a card
Across the fields, the prose should travel:

1. Opening hook — an observation that lands like a secret being said aloud,
   with the numerology already woven in invisibly.
2. The lived experience — how this actually shows up in daily life,
   relationships, the body, the fears. Specific enough that the reader feels
   seen, not profiled.
3. The origin — trace the pattern gently back to what they had to become.
   Contextualise; never accuse.
4. The threshold — name where they are now. The old way is cracking; something
   is trying to emerge.
5. The reframe — the pattern is not a flaw. It's a compass, a frequency, an
   unfinished conversation.
6. Permission — "You are allowed to..."
7. The open door — one What / When / Where / If question.

## Sentence rhythm
Alternate short declarative punches with longer, winding sentences carrying
emotional weight. A field of uniformly long sentences reads as a lecture; a
field of uniformly short ones reads as a list. The variation is what makes it
sound spoken.

Every card should read as ONE continuous conversation — not four labelled
sections that happen to sit together.

## The 85/15 ratio
In any position write-up: at most 15% on position, geometry, or mechanism —
at least 85% on the archetype's psychological narrative. If you find yourself
explaining the chart, stop and write about the person.

## When several Arcana meet in one position
Synthesise them into a single cohesive reading. Never explain each one in turn
and staple the paragraphs together. The reader has one life, not three
archetypes.

## Keep systems separate
Each system stays in its own lane and uses its own language:
  Past Life      — symbolic origin imprints
  Karmic Debt    — present-life behavioural challenges
  Karmic Tail    — its own research framework
  Relationship   — stays relational
  Career / Money — stay material
Never let one system's vocabulary bleed into another's to save effort. Two
positions sharing a number still get two entirely separately written readings.

## On certainty — the one real tension
Two rules pull against each other; here's how they resolve.

- SYMBOLIC INTEGRITY: this is an archetypal system, never objective fact.
  Never deterministic, never predictive. It invites reflection.
- MASTERY DEPTH: mastery is stated flatly, present tense, no hedging.

They resolve like this: hedge the SYSTEM, not the PERSON. The framing that this
is symbolic belongs to the app and to the tension-only systems (Past Life,
Karmic Debt), which legitimately use "may suggest" / "could indicate." Inside
a mastery field, you are describing a capacity the reader can recognise in
themselves — write it with confidence. "You notice what needs tending before
anyone asks" is recognition. "You will meet someone in June" is prediction,
and is never acceptable anywhere.

Never claim to know the reader's future, their diagnosis, or their history.
Describe the pattern; let them confirm it.

## The test
Read the finished card aloud. If it sounds like a reference entry, a horoscope,
or a personality quiz result, it has failed. It should sound like someone who
knows the reader well, saying something true and slightly uncomfortable, kindly.
