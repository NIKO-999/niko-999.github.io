Reminders

options · nothing here is saved

Same four reminders in every one. The tension worth naming: colour came off the habits and the check-in because a losing day is a judgement about you. A reminder is not that — it is a task with a date, and being told the dentist was Tuesday is information rather than a verdict. So colour is allowed here, but it is earned by urgency, never appears on one that is simply not done yet, and there is only ever one loud row on the screen.
