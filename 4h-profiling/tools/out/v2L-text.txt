A candle is made up of:
Wick = Reversal
Body = Expansion
Price expands after reversal.
The goal is simple: identify the reversal, and then trade the expansion.
Phases of Price Delivery in a Candle

[page 5]
We’re basically trading away from the daily high/low (which becomes the wick), toward the Draw on Liquidity.
A day has 6 × 4H candles.
If reversal happens in the early candles (18:00 / 22:00 / 2:00), it’s an Overnight/London reversal, and NY session
becomes the expansion phase.
If the high/low isn’t formed until the NY session (6:00 / 10:00), then that’s your NY reversal.
Price moves from:
High Resistance Liquidity Runs (HRLR) → Low Resistance Liquidity Runs (LRLR)
 In simple terms: manipulation → failure swing.

If the previous 4H candle has a reversal aligned with the daily profile and phases of price, then the new 4H candle will
expand.
How to Engage:
Inside the 4H candle, if there is a 1H reversal candle established, then trade the continuation.
If no swing point exists from the previous 4H candle, then wait for the new 4H candle to create that swing and
continue.
If the daily candle is still in the protraction phase, then wait for the new 4H candle to reverse and expand.
You can use the classic MMXM entries or the TTFM model to enter these setups.
Scalping Entries: 4H → 1H → M5/M3
