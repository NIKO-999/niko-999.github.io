Daily Range Structure
Eleven_Trades
A daily range comprises six 4H candles. The focus should be on three key candles:
For Indices:
2:00 AM - 6:00 AM
6:00 AM - 10:00 AM
10:00 AM - 2:00 PM (Better to close trades before 12:00 PM NY Lunch).
For Forex:
1:00 AM - 5:00 AM
5:00 AM - 9:00 AM
9:00 AM - 1:00 PM (On days with 10:00 AM news; close trades before 12:00 PM).

4H Candle Profiling Patterns
Eleven_Trades
Asia Reversal Pattern:
If Asia candles reverse, expect London expansion and NY continuation.
Indices: Reversal likely within the 18:00 or 22:00 candle.
Forex: Reversal likely within the 17:00 or 21:00 candle.
London Reversal Pattern:
On a classic buy day, expect London to reverse and NY to continue expanding.
Indices: Reversal occurs within the 2:00 AM - 6:00 AM window (focus on the 2:00 AM- 4H candle).
Forex: Reversal occurs within the 1:00 AM - 5:00 AM window (focus on the 1:00 AM-4H candle).

NY Reversal Patterns
When London and Asia Fail:
If both London and Asia sessions fail to reverse or manipulate, NY candles are expected to handle reversals and
continuations. This is more common on news days.
Indices:
6:00 AM - 10:00 AM candle acts as a reversal into expansion.
6:00 AM candle (reversal occurs within the 6:00 - 10:00 AM window, often during news) followed by a 10:00 AM
continuation.
If the 6:00am 4H candle fails to reverse, then expect the 10:00 candle to manipulate and initiate the Expansion.
Forex:
5:00 AM - 9:00 AM candle acts as a reversal into expansion.
If 5:00 AM - 9:00 AM fails to reverse/manipulate, the 9:00 AM candle will handle the reversal into expansion.
