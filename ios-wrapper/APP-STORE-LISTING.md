**Description:**
Lift is a workout log that gets out of your way.

Log a set at a time with last time's weight and reps right beside it. A smart suggestion tells you what to try next, and one tap fills it in.

- A calendar of every day you trained
- Rest timer that keeps going with the screen off
- Plans for the week, warm-ups and drop sets
- Personal records and streaks
- Badges to earn, from bronze up to mythic
- Share a clean card of your workout
- Friends: compare how often you train, never what you lifted

Your workouts stay on your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking. Friends is optional and off until you turn it on.
